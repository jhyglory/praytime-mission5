# PrayTime product

PrayTime helps adult Protestant Christians pause before reflexively opening SNS, pray and deliberately decide whether to return or use the app briefly. The landing explains the product and collects beta reservations; reservation is free and does not guarantee immediate access or beta participation.

## Core flow

**PAUSE → PRAY → CHOOSE → USE → RETURN**

Opening a configured app triggers a pause. After personal prayer or a prayer guide, users choose RETURN or 5 / 10 / 15 minutes of intentional use. Prayer alone does not unlock SNS. Restrictions return when the selected time expires; additional use requires another prayer/choice.

Do not promise unconfirmed capabilities or quantified outcomes. No faith scoring, prayer ranking, streaks, AI prayer/STT, competition or paywall. Prayer originals are described as not collected by design direction, not as an independently verified future-product guarantee.
